# Iron Front: Generals' War

A browser real-time strategy game in the style of classic Generals-era RTS games. It has 4 factions and supports up to 8 players, online or against AI, on phone or PC.

**Play:** https://rblmarrero-sketch.github.io/iron-front/

## Factions
- **USA**: high-tech units and a strong air force. Superweapon: Orbital Lance.
- **China**: cheap armour that gets a horde bonus when grouped, plus Cyber Operative hackers. Superweapon: Doomsday nuclear silo.
- **Russia**: fortified buildings, heavy tanks, snipers and Storm rocket artillery. Superweapon: Blizzard Strike.
- **Free Militia**: guerrilla units with no power needs. Wrecked vehicles leave salvage that upgrades their units. Superweapon: Hellfire Storm.

## Modes
- **Skirmish**: up to 8 slots, each a human or an AI (Easy, Normal or Hard). Play free-for-all or in teams of up to 4.
- **Online**: the host clicks Host and shares the link, and friends join. Players connect directly through WebRTC (PeerJS), so no accounts are needed.
- **Maps**: Small to Huge, in Temperate, Desert or Snow. They include lakes, capturable oil derricks and supply-drop crates.

## Controls
- **PC**: choose Classic (left-click to command) or Modern (right-click to command). Mouse-wheel zooms. Hotkeys include A, S, Ctrl+A, Q–I, Z–B, G and 1–9.
- **Phone/tablet**: tap to select or command, drag to pan, pinch to zoom, and long-press to box-select. Side buttons cover the main actions.
