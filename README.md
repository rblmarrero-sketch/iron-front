# Iron Front: Generals' War

A browser real-time strategy game in the style of classic Generals-era RTS games. It has three asymmetric factions, aircraft, upgrades, General powers and superweapons. You can play against the AI or online 1v1.

**Play:** https://rblmarrero-sketch.github.io/iron-front/

## Online 1v1
1. Click **Host online game**, then **Copy link** and send the link to your friend.
2. Your friend opens the link, picks a faction and clicks **Join by code**.

No accounts are needed. Players connect directly to each other through WebRTC, using PeerJS. Both players should use the same browser, for example both Chrome.

## Factions
- **Coalition**: high-tech units and a strong air force. Superweapon: Orbital Lance.
- **Red Legion**: cheap armor and infantry that get a horde bonus when grouped. Superweapon: Doomsday nuclear silo.
- **Free Militia**: guerrilla units with no power needs. Wrecked vehicles leave salvage that upgrades their units. Superweapon: Hellfire Storm.
